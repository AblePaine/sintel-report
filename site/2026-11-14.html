Past 24h overview#

Agent traces are a credential store — Cursor's breach started with a key issued to an inference vendor#

  • Cursor's November 13 incident update traces its October breach to an access key Cursor had issued to Fireworks AI, its inference vendor, which Cursor says was exposed at Fireworks. The attacker used it to copy historical coding-agent traces from a read-only shared bucket — mostly 2025 sessions outside Privacy Mode, under 0.05% of users — and a small number of traces contained unredacted credentials (API credentials for under 0.01% of customers). From there the attacker reached internal SaaS tools and, on October 29, placed tampered copies of Anthropic's Claude Code extension (versions 2.1.312/2.1.313) into Cursor's marketplace cache for about 11 hours, reaching at most 5,000 users who requested those versions. Cursor says production infrastructure, signing keys, customer repositories and the upstream Open VSX marketplace were not affected, last attacker activity was November 1, and all inference has moved off Fireworks. One attribution caveat stands: Fireworks' own November 9 bulletin does not mention Cursor. The structural point: agent session traces — prompts, tool calls, terminal output — are now a proven secrets-leak surface, because agents routinely touch live credentials in the clear. Agents got a card-network wallet and a contract ban in the same 48 hours#

  • OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on November 13: its resident "dots" agents can complete purchases using network-issued one-time tokens, built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, with a security check on every purchase and the real card number never exposed to the agent or the merchant. Rollout starts with US ChatGPT Pro subscribers, 18+, holding eligible US-issued cards, inside dot conversations only; OpenAI is taking no transaction cut at launch, per CNBC reporting. The counter-move is already in contract law: United Airlines' November 11 Contract of Carriage revision (Rule 6.J.6) makes using an AI agent to make, modify or service a ticket a prohibited practice — even when the passenger supervises and approves every step — and Rule 5.H lets United cancel agent-handled reservations without notice; bookings made before November 11 are honoured. United's CEO told the Wall Street Journal he did not want "to create another middleman." Overnight claims that Amazon, Walmart, Target and eBay are imposing similar blocks are unverified single-source posts, not confirmed policy. The split is the story: the card networks are building a sanctioned agent door with tokens and per-purchase checks, while a major merchant is using contract terms to keep any door from existing until it controls the terms.   • The Federal Reserve's November Financial Stability Report, published November 13, puts AI at the top of its market-contact survey: 87% of 23 contacts cited AI as a salient risk, up from 50% in spring and 30% in fall 2025, ahead of a risk-asset correction (52%) and higher long-term rates (48%). Edge signal#

Commerce split into two webs in 48 hours — and the split is over who issues the permission. On November 13 the card networks gave agents a wallet: one-time tokens, a check on every purchase, no card number anywhere an agent can leak it. On November 11 United wrote agents into its contract as a prohibited practice, cancellable without notice, supervised or not — and its CEO named the reason: no new middleman. Schwab's approval-gated MCP, Brave's per-query x402 pricing and now Wallet are all the same invention from different sides: a door with a named issuer, a scoped token and a check at the threshold. The ban is what a merchant does before it has a door of its own. Everything in this window points the same way — Cursor's traces leaking credentials an agent was trusted with, the Fed counting $3T of obligations behind the buildout, DeepSeek repricing the tools — the scarce asset is no longer capability or even access. It is verifiable permission: who authorised this agent, for what, under whose token, provable afterwards. Builds that issue, check or record that permission — for payments, for trades, for tool contracts, for traces — are standing where the toll is moving. Builds that merely act are about to start asking at doors.
