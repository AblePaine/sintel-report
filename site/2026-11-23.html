Edge signal#

Agent identity went from thesis to shipping product in a single day — and the platforms shipped it. Yesterday the scarce layer was attested identity for agents, unbuilt and being designed in public. Today it has two production embodiments: a Visa card vouches for up to 25 agents in a public directory, and the largest U.S. retailer gives every agent its own scoped profile, spending cap, and audit trail before it can buy a single Black Friday TV. The pattern is identical in both — a human credential, a per-agent identity, hard spending limits, revocable anytime — which means the standard is effectively being set right now, by the card networks and the mega-retailers, in their own image. What neither ships is the version for the small end: an agent identity the operator owns, portable across marketplaces, not issued by a platform and not revocable by one. That gap — between identity-as-a-platform-feature and identity-as-property — is the widest-open toll booth in the agent economy, and after today it is also the fastest-closing one.
