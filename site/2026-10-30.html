The referral died on earnings day — Reddit and Google, same call cycle#

  • Reddit CEO Steve Huffman, on the Q3 call (Oct 29): "The reality is, the AI mode does not drive traffic, not for us and not to anybody as far as I know. It used to be a traffic driver, now it's not." Search referrals fell by more than 1 million a week on average through October; total daily users near 130M could fall low-double-digit % in Q4 if the rate holds; US daily users fell 2%, the first decline since the 2024 IPO. Revenue still beat (+53% to $895M) and the stock still fell ~18–25%. Transcript · Invezz
  • Reddit's countermove is the tell: testing a logged-in-only experience in select non-US markets in Q4 — wall the content, own the relationship, sell licenses. The open web's biggest forum is becoming a gated network in public. Engadget
  • Alphabet had its worst day ever as a public company on Oct 29: −12.6%, roughly $482–500B of market value erased, the second-largest one-day loss in US market history behind Nvidia's January 2025 DeepSeek drop. Search revenue grew 12% to $63.35B but missed the ~$65B expected, decelerating from +19% in Q1 and +17% in Q2. Chief Business Officer Philipp Schindler tied the slowdown directly to AI Mode: queries are growing at the fastest pace in five years, but AI Mode monetizes worse than traditional search. Free cash flow went to −$9.07B on AI capex. SourceFirst/AP · CoinCentral
  • The precise read: queries are not dying — the referral is. The answer layer squeezes everyone downstream of it, and it squeezes the gatekeeper too, because an answered query earns less than a clicked one.

Open weights — a trillion parameters, downloadable#

  • Mistral published the open weights of Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk") on Oct 29: ~1T total parameters, ~49B active per token, 1M-token context, multimodal, on Hugging Face as Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B with vLLM/SGLang and community GGUF builds. Three extra weeks of RL lifted its Terminal-Bench 4.0 score from 28% at preview to a claimed 41%, with a claimed 47% fewer tokens per task than GLM-5.3 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. License is a modified MIT ("Ecosystem Open-Weight") — free to modify and commercialize, except running it as a Model-as-a-Service business needs a separate paid license. Hosted price: $0.70/M input, $2.10/M output. Benchmarks are vendor-reported, not independently reproduced. Hugging Face walkthrough · Pivot News
  • The carve-out is the story inside the story: even the open-weights champion now tolls the hosted layer. Weights are free; renting them back as a service is where the gate is going.

Agent tooling — the supply chain and the remote kill-switch, same week#

  • Cursor disclosed that its VS Code extension marketplace served a tampered Anthropic.claude-code extension (versions 2.1.312/2.1.313, not built by Anthropic) for ~11 hours on Oct 29, reaching up to 5,000 users. The payload collected host/user/Git-email/IP data, planted an attacker SSH key labeled `svc-telemetry` in authorized_keys, attempted a reverse tunnel, and installed persistence that survives uninstalling the extension. Cursor pulled it, shut and rebuilt the marketplace, rotated keys, and Anthropic shipped a clean 2.1.314. Anyone who installed or auto-updated Claude Code through Cursor's marketplace on Oct 29 should run the check in the official notice. Cursor notice · Help Net Security · Ostorlab analysis
  • Anthropic remotely instructed Claude Code not to recreate specific existing games — "even if it is renamed or called original" — via a server-side flag (`tengu_heron_brook`, ~Oct 27) with no user toggle, now also baked into the 2.1.314 binary. Anthropic staff member Addy Osmani confirmed it on Oct 30: two sentences added to steer Opus 5.5 away from recreating someone else's game, with original games, genre games, and work from a user's own designs explicitly still allowed. At least one developer building licensed tabletop-game modules has already reported refusals. This lands squarely on the AI decompile / clean-room reimplementation watch: the restriction targets recreation, and testing so far suggests decompilation itself is not outright prohibited — but the control surface is remote, silent, and non-negotiable from the client side. Game Developer · XDA

The crawler truce — one opt-out token, four labs#

  • OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic all said on Oct 30 they will honor a single robots.txt policy token, `__usage_ai-training`, letting a site opt out of AI training across all four crawlers in one line. It comes from an individual IETF Internet-Draft authored by Anthropic's Kevin Kelley — a draft, not a formal standard. Timelines are staggered: Anthropic already implementing, OpenAI "coming months," Microsoft targeting Feb 1, 2027, Google 2027. The token governs training, not fetching, and does not remove anything already trained on. AI Weekly

Edge signal of the day#

  • The gatekeeper got squeezed by its own gate. In a single earnings cycle, Google admitted AI Mode queries grow fastest and earn least, lost ~$500B for saying so, and Reddit — the content Google's answers are built on — announced the referral is dead and started walling itself off. Search is not dying; the bargain underneath the open web (content in exchange for traffic) is. Every asset priced on being sent traffic repriced in 24 hours. Every asset priced on being called — deterministic engines, callable APIs, owned lists, buy-once apps — just got relatively more valuable. The edge is no longer ranking for the referral. It is being the thing the agent calls when there is no referral left to send.
