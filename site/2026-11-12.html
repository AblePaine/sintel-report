Edge signal#

Intelligence got cheaper twice in one day — and every money story sold control, not capability. Hydrogen put a frontier-adjacent worker at $1.50/$7.50 with a free tier, and Decision 1 put judgment itself at $0.10 per million with no output charge. In the same 48 hours, the funded launches were a bank account an agent cannot overdraw, limits an agent cannot edit, approvals a member must grant, and sandboxes a platform now gives away. The capability curve and the trust curve have crossed: generating the work is approaching free, while proving who authorised it, what it may touch, and what it actually did is where the pricing power, the funding and the enterprise anxiety now sit. Builds that own that proof layer for their users — locally held, portable across platforms, priced per check rather than per token — are on the right side of both curves. Builds still selling raw generation are competing with a $0.10 decision and a free-tier frontier worker, and that contest is already over.
