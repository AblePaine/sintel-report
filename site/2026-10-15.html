Past 24h overview#

Open weights — the day's headline#

  • Meta released Muse Spark Open (Oct 15). 816B total parameters, 42B active, up to 1M tokens of context, multimodal (text, image, video, audio), positioned for coding and agentic work. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model; its own tables put it ahead of Qwen3.8, GLM 5.3, and Kimi K3 on several agentic and reasoning rows, and well behind the closed Muse Wonder Full. The reference local run is two paired M5 Ultra Mac Studios with 512GB unified memory each — this is a datacenter-and-desktop-pair model, not a single-GPU model. Hosted access is live via Baseten, Fireworks, Groq, Modal, Nebius, OpenRouter, and Together. Reuters VentureBeat Edge: the open/closed gap is now a tier, not a chasm — Meta keeps the flagship rented and gives away the tier just below it. That is the commoditise-the-complement play run against both OpenAI and Anthropic at once.
  • The licence is the gate — weights ship under the Muse Model License, not MIT/Apache: a prominent "Built with Muse Spark Open" notice is required, and any business offering the model as a service past $20M in aggregate revenue needs a separate written agreement with Meta. The Hugging Face download gate also asks for full legal name, date of birth, and full organisation name. HF licence Edge, watch-list (self-host rights): the weights are genuinely downloadable and fine-tunable, but the toll booth moved into the licence and the identity gate. Open, with a registry at the door.
  • Also open today: Black Forest Labs' FLUX 3 Image Klein — 5.6B-parameter open-weight image model, native 4K, editing with up to ten reference images; BFL claims top open-model Elo in its internal testing. The Decoder Edge: the open media stack keeps filling in under the hosted generators, one modality at a time.
