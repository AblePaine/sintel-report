Agent infrastructure / protocols#

  • Personal Agent Protocol (PAP / "Poppy") draft 0.1 is the real story under the Gemini scan lead — announced Oct 6 by Sierra + Meta, draft published Oct 9, with 35 additional design partners including OpenAI, Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal. Python/TypeScript SDKs already on PyPI; no reference implementation and no payments in v0.1 yet. Oct 12–13 brought the explainer/SMB wave. dev.to explainer Data Studios Edge: PAP is the identity/session layer that sits above the payment rails — the front door every agent-consumable business will eventually need to publish.
