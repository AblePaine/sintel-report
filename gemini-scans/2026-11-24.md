* **Arbitrage Zero-Copy Institutional Data Feeds:** Moody’s pushing zero-copy intelligence directly into agent toolchains via MCP and Snowflake signals that raw enterprise data vendors are bypassing traditional API monetization. Developers can capitalize by building vertical-specific agent runtimes pre-integrated with these zero-copy stores, allowing mid-market financial, legal, and compliance firms to deploy autonomous research agents that query institutional datasets instantly without paying per-query SaaS markup.
* **Launch Public-Market Comp Infrastructure for Embodied AI:** Following Agility's Nasdaq debut (`AGLT`) and massive capital inflows into humanoid and physical AI infrastructure, public investors lack pure-play tooling indexes. Builders should focus on simulation-to-real testing pipelines, synthetic data generation wrappers for edge robotics, and standardized fleet-management dashboards tailored for companies transitioning from pilot stage to public market accountability.
