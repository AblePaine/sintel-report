### Ways to Capitalize

* **Build Workflow-Specific "Skills" Packs for the AAIF Transition:** Now that *Agent Skills* are governed neutrally under the Linux Foundation and integrated into over 70 platforms (Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT), enterprise software vendors should immediately package their proprietary operational workflows into standardized `SKILL.md` libraries. Build vertical-specific skills (e.g., medical billing compliance, automated tax filing) and monetize distribution via agent-directory ecosystems before open-source commoditization flattens wrapper tools.
* **Exploit the Agent-to-Agent Ad Economy (AdCP):** Traditional media buying dashboards are being bypassed by machine-to-machine protocols like AdCP. Indie developers can capture massive margin by building "Publisher MCP Proxies"—lightweight middleware that exposes niche publisher inventory, newsletters, and programmatic audio feeds directly to AI buying agents using standard tool calls, capturing a cut of zero-UI transactions.
* **Arbitrage the Developer CI Bottleneck:** With AI coding agents multiplying pull requests exponentially, traditional CI testing pipelines are buckling under the volume (as evidenced by Blacksmith's explosive growth). Build specialized, lightweight test runner agents that execute deterministic unit tests locally or pre-filter code smells *before* committing heavy jobs to centralized CI infrastructure, undercutting expensive cloud testing bills.
