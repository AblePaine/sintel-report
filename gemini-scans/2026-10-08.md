### Ways to Capitalize
* **Build Agent-First SaaS Wrappers for Legacy Protocols:** Traditional mailboxes, ticketing systems, and CRM tools are fundamentally ill-equipped for programmatic, multi-hop sub-agents. There is wide-open leverage in building headless, API-native, and cryptographically attributable communication layers (such as MCP-native mail and task queues) that enterprises can drop into automated workflows without risking shadow-IT sprawl or breaking NIST API-inventory compliance.
* **Arbitrage the "Opex-to-CapEx" Open-Weights Shift:** With Mistral's Le Chonk and Reflection's Beam bringing massive 500B+ parameter models into open weights, the margin profile of renting frontier closed APIs for routine autonomous coding and security workflows is collapsing. Builders should pivot service offerings toward on-premise/private-cloud distillation and fine-tuning pipelines, helping enterprises "own" their models on single-node hardware configurations rather than paying continuous per-token rents.
