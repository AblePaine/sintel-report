Ways to capitalize:
* **Build Authorized Adapters for the Personal Agent Protocol:** With major retailers and rails (Shopify, Stripe, Walmart) committing to Meta and Sierra’s Personal Agent Protocol, standard web scrapers and brittle browser-automation wrappers face rapid obsolescence. Build middleware that lets mid-market e-commerce, SaaS, and service platforms instantly expose secure, protocol-native endpoints and OAuth capability scopes for consumer AI agents. 
* **Target the Biopharma "Autonomous Lab" Stack:** Drylab's traction in automated scientific discovery proves that regulated domains will pay for localized agent swarms that interface directly with internal databases. Opportunity: Build verification-and-audit layers for scientific agents that automatically generate compliance trails required by the FDA or EMA for automated drug discovery.
* **Exploit the Embodied AI Data Bottleneck:** With physical AI labs sitting on tens of billions in fresh capital, the primary constraint is no longer compute—it is high-fidelity, real-world manipulation data from actual workplaces. Build tooling, synthetic-to-real simulation pipelines, or secure capture hardware tailored to record specialized manual labor for robotics training sets.
