### Ways to capitalize
* **Build Cross-Hardware Abstraction Layers for Physical AI:** With labs like Dream Labs proving that cross-robot zero-shot transfer via video training is viable, the real bottleneck is no longer unique model training per chassis, but clean tooling that translates unified action outputs across disparate actuator interfaces and gripper types. Build the middleware adapter layer for low-cost, multi-vendor robotic fleets.
* **Capitalize on the Tactile Data Deficit:** As companies like Enact prioritize internal motor-force feedback over camera-only setups, there is a glaring lack of structured touch, torque, and slip-resistance datasets. Build telemetry-logging wrappers for industrial machinery and commercial grippers to capture the next scarce training modality.
* **Enterprise MCP Gateway Auditing & Compliance:** As more non-tech entities like Notified expose core databases to agentic protocols, enterprise risk officers will face severe governance hurdles regarding unauthorized tool calls and data leakage. Build lightweight authorization proxies that sit in front of MCP servers to enforce strict OAuth policies and row-level data boundaries.
