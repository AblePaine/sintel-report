### Ways to capitalize
* **Build Stateless MCP Security & Routing Proxies:** With MCP now operating statelessly via HTTP headers (`Mcp-Method`, `Mcp-Name`), enterprise IT needs specialized edge gateways that can inspect, authorize, and rate-limit agent-to-server traffic *without* maintaining sticky sessions. The leverage is in building the "Cloudflare Worker or NGINX module" equivalent for agentic traffic governance.
* **Launch Robotic-Failure Audit & Simulation Suites:** As embodied AI models push into physical manufacturing and logistics, the bottleneck has shifted from model training to failure-mode verification. Building sandboxed simulation pipelines that automatically subject foundation control policies to edge-case adversarial physics (similar to web fuzzing, but for atoms) is an open wide-space market for indie dev teams.
* **Displace Legacy EDA Verification Engineers:** The operational proof that semiconductor agents can compress weeks of verification loops into minutes means the leverage in chip design is shifting entirely to prompt and constraint architects. Software builders should target vertical niches (e.g., custom IoT ASICs) where a 2-person team using agentic EDA flows can out-design a legacy 50-person engineering shop.
