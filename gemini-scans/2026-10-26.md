# Gemini morning scan — 2026-10-26

* **NVIDIA Unveils OpenShell Reference Architecture for Secure Enterprise Agents:** NVIDIA announced a full-stack infrastructure design combining Vera Rubin hardware, Vera CPUs, and BlueField DPUs running the Sentry security framework to provide out-of-band behavioral monitoring and in-silicon policy enforcement for autonomous enterprise agents. Source: [Engineering.com](https://www.engineering.com/nvidia-unveils-architecture-for-secure-enterprise-agents/)
* **Marrow Secures $2M Pre-Seed for Regulated Insurance Agent Rails:** London-based InsurTech Marrow raised funding led by Insurtech Gateway to scale its AMI Standards—a machine-readable protocol letting AI assistants safely query, compare, and bind regulated insurance policies on behalf of consumers without bypassing carrier compliance. Source: [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/marrow-raises-2m-to-bring-ai-agents-into-insurance-sales/)
* **Enact Intelligence Launches with Record Swiss Pre-Seed:** Zurich-based Enact emerged from stealth with a $30 million pre-seed round co-led by redalpine and Notion Capital, backed by Yann LeCun and Arthur Mensch, to build tactile-sensing foundation models and software for industrial robotics. Source: [TNW via Tech.eu](https://www.tech.eu/)

Ways to capitalize:
* **Build Agent-Native Compliance Wrappers for Regulated Verticals:** As Marrow’s traction with carriers like Aviva shows, horizontal agent payment layers fail when compliance, disclosures, and binding rules enter the loop. There is massive leverage in building verticalized translation middleware for highly regulated sectors (wealth management, healthcare, logistics) that packages enterprise core systems into secure, protocol-compliant endpoints for third-party AI assistants.
* **Develop In-Silicon & Out-of-Band Agent Governance Guardrails:** With NVIDIA baking behavioral enforcement directly into BlueField DPUs via OpenShell, the enterprise software market is signaling that application-layer guardrails are dead. Builders should target hardware-accelerated runtime monitoring, creating security audit tools that interface directly with DPU telemetry to catch recursive agent loops and data-exfiltration attempts before execution.
