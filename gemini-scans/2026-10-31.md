### Ways to Capitalize

* **Build Automated Opt-Out Compliance Auditing:** With major labs implementing unified publisher blocking tools, publishers and media networks will struggle to verify whether their blocks are respected across various wrapper APIs, localized scrapers, and agent frameworks. Build a compliance verification service that continuously tests and crawls client digital properties using randomized agent signatures to audit whether AI labs are respecting exclusion rules.
* **Pivot from Scraping Infrastructures to Agent-Readable APIs:** As anti-bot walls tighten and standardized blocking takes effect, traditional brute-force DOM scraping via headless browsers faces severe degradation. The high-margin opportunity shifts to providing clean, machine-readable micro-APIs with built-in micro-payment settlement (e.g., via x402 or agent-native rails) that allow autonomous agents to buy legal, structured data payloads directly instead of navigating hostile user interfaces.
* **Enterprise MCP Tool Governance and RBAC Security Layers:** The formalization of "Skills over MCP" means internal corporate tools and prompt instructions will increasingly be bundled and streamed dynamically to agents. Build enterprise middleware that intercepts MCP tool execution to enforce real-time Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), preventing malicious or hallucinating agents from executing unauthorized multi-step workflows over enterprise connectors.
