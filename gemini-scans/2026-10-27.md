### Ways to Capitalize
* **Build Programmatic Revocation and Dynamic Spend-Guardrails for Agent Wallets:** Most agent infrastructure focuses on issuance and static permissions; the immediate enterprise bottleneck is *revocation* and real-time budget enforcement. Build middleware that acts as a cryptographic circuit-breaker, enabling instant authority termination and programmatic velocity limits for machine-to-machine stablecoin transactions.
* **Launch "Dark Data" Capture Operations for Physical AI:** With robotics capital concentrated heavily on foundation movement models (Skild, Physical Intelligence), text and internet scrapes are useless. The leverage belongs to operators who can legally instrument niche commercial, industrial, or domestic environments with multi-camera rigs to aggregate verified manipulation and force-feedback datasets. 
* **Package Open-Weights Reasoning Models into Stateless, Edge-Compliant Microservices:** Leverage permissive 30B-class open releases (such as Google’s Gemma 4 or Meta's Muse Glimmer) to build ultra-low-latency vertical assistants that execute locally on consumer hardware or edge clouds, bypassing the high margin-extraction and latency of frontier closed-API wrappers.
