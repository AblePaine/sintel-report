## Ways to capitalize

- **Provider attestation is now a product.** The OpenRouter incident shows that a "ZDR" flag on a model router is only a claim, not a guarantee. Build a canary-as-a-service: plant a tracked link in each request to every provider and publish a live leak and retention scoreboard. Sell it to routers and enterprise buyers. The router that can show TEE or attested inference wins regulated traffic. Unverifiable resellers of Chinese models get squeezed.
- **Local-first agent stacks become real at 26B.** Inkling 2 Mini is Apache-licensed, fits in 16GB and does about 125 tok/s on a MacBook. Package it as a turnkey offline coding and ops agent for firms that can't send code to the cloud, such as law, defense or healthcare IT. Sell it as a one-time license plus skills packs, not per token. Small-tier API margins are what this displaces.
- **Capital is concentrating in agent apps, not infrastructure.** Cognition at $75B and Databricks at $225B in private markets, in the same week the Nasdaq 100 fell 10% and Oracle felt it had to post that it's solvent, means money is betting on owning the workflow over the compute. The play for a builder: vertical agents that own a whole job end to end (like Devin owns tickets), priced on outcomes.
- **Agent checkout before Black Friday.** If Stripe's agent-wallet numbers hold, travel and retail merchants need agent-readable catalogs and policy endpoints this week. A plug-in that makes a Shopify or travel inventory agent-purchasable through Link or stablecoin rails is a quick-turn consulting offer.
- **Agent-shaped robotics evals.** Waddle-1 wins partly by keeping memory across trials. That's an agent-harness advantage, not a model advantage. The leverage is in robot-use harnesses that sit on top of any frontier model. Benchmarks that control for that difference are their own open niche.
