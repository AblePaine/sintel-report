## Ways to capitalize

- **Patch-to-exploit is now hours, so sell the time between disclosure and patching.** Build an agent that reads your customers' dependency updates the moment they ship, ranks which fixes can be exploited, and auto-opens the upgrade PR or WAF rule. Corma's round shows buyers are paying for this now. Displaces: monthly patch cycles and human vuln triage.
- **Skill QA is the new package-registry gap.** NVIDIA open-sourced the evaluator, but nobody runs the trusted registry of signed, scanned and scored skills. A "skills CDN" served over MCP (the reShapr pattern), with a security score on every listing, is what npm should have been. This fits the SideBand skill lane directly.
- **Agent-facing storefronts are table stakes.** Product Hunt shipping MCP with OAuth means directories now compete for agent traffic, not human clicks. Any niche directory (tools, vendors, grants) without an MCP endpoint is about to lose its referral flow.
- **Liability turns into a product.** The CA suit puts containment logs and an incident count in the record. Sell agent-containment audit trails (sandbox, egress logs, kill-switch proof) to labs and enterprises before regulators set the format.
- **The AI-infra credit crack is a second signal.** A discounted Jupiter loan plus a run-rate brag made at the expense of the "company going public this week" point to capital-efficient, smaller players. Build on cheap open-weights routing (HF and OpenRouter, Sarvam), not on hyperscaler lock-in.
