## Ways to capitalize

- **Decision calls went to zero in 48 hours.** Google priced closed-set scoring at $0.10/M; Perplexity answered with Apache weights, free. Don't sell the scoring. Sell what sits on it: per-customer calibration, the decision log, and audit of why an agent routed or refused. Anyone reselling LLM-as-judge per check is now displaced.
- **MCP needs a lockfile.** 94 tools vanishing in a day and OpenAI's directory catching credential-taking schemas mean agent fleets break or leak silently. Build the pin-and-diff layer: snapshot every server's tool contract, alert on change, run contract tests in CI, block credential fields. Sell to teams running agents in production, priced per server.
- **The evidence bundle is the new PR.** Expo records simulator runs, AWS ships an emulator, Anthropic lets the harness run inside your perimeter. Review is moving from reading diffs to checking proof. Build the reviewer that collects recordings, test runs and receipts from every sandbox into one pass/fail card per agent change.
- **Agents are calling businesses; businesses need a line for agents.** Muse, Fish Agent and Retell's 100M calls a month mean front desks will get AI callers at volume. Build the agent-facing receiving side: a phone number that detects an AI caller and hands off to a structured endpoint (MCP or API) for bookings, claims and status checks. Call centers and IVR vendors get squeezed.
- **NVIDIA's backstop is less certain.** A cautious CFO as CEO, a subpoena threat and earnings on Nov 17 put the neocloud take-or-pay floors and lease guarantees in question. Neoclouds that relied on NVIDIA as the credit anchor face tighter terms; builders who own capacity contracts outright, or broker spare GB300 time, gain leverage. Watch the Nov 17 call for any change to the program.
