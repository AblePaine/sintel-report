## Ways to capitalize

- **Identity is the new distribution for MCP.** Claude vouching for its users means MCP servers that accept the assertion become zero-click installs across every Team and Enterprise org. Most small MCP builders can't implement the JWT-bearer and ID-JAG exchange. Build a drop-in "accept Claude and IdP assertions" auth proxy and sell it per server. The losers are connectors that still make users go through an OAuth screen.
- **Agent permissions are now a funded category.** Sentra's $150M plus CrowdStrike's deals yesterday say enterprises will pay to know what each agent can touch. The SMB version doesn't exist yet: an agent-access audit for Google Workspace and M365 that lists every connected agent and its scopes and one-clicks revoke. Sell it through MSPs.
- **The repo is the agent's filesystem, and the labs want to own it.** Pando, then Pierre: OpenAI is moving under GitHub. The opening is Git-backed storage for things other than code: agent memory files, contracts, design artifacts, all versioned and diffable for agents. That's Pierre's pitch, and after this deal it's an orphaned market for everyone not on Codex.
- **Sandboxes are going to zero.** At about $5 a month, a persistent per-user agent is viable for consumer apps. Margin moves to model calls and distribution. Build consumer "always-on" agents now, because the infra cost objection just went away. Hosted-sandbox vendors with fat margins (Vercel, Daytona) face pricing pressure.
- **Open media weights with geo-fenced licenses create a hosting arbitrage.** P-H3-Image is free outside the US, EU, UK and Korea. Meanwhile fal's raise shows hosted media inference is still a huge business on convenience. A non-US host serving licensed open media models to those markets can price under fal and Replicate.
