## Ways to capitalize
- **Hyperscaler sale-leasebacks make AI capex a yield product.** After Meta/Blue Owl, Amazon/BXDC confirms the pattern: hyperscalers recycle capital through REITs. The leverage for builders is in the layer below: tools for powered-shell diligence (interconnect queues, power contracts, lease abstracts) that every REIT, lender and ratings shop now needs. Expect smaller hyperscaler-tenant REITs and private credit vehicles to copy this.
- **The price floor for "good enough" agent tokens just fell again.** Hy4 Flash at $0.07/M input, plus Qwen4 small weights on the way, means routine agent loops (triage, extraction, browsing) should run on near-free models, with a frontier model only as a supervisor. Build a router or harness that does this by default and sell the savings; flat-fee agent products get much better margins. Who's displaced: Haiku- and Flash-tier pricing power.
- **Skills are becoming deployable, governed artifacts.** FastMCP serves skills from the server, NVIDIA scores them, and PayPal reviews, versions and ships them as plugins. That's the 1997 "web server + CDN + app store" stack forming for skills. Open lanes: a hosted skill registry with eval scores and signing, a skill-security scanner (injection inside SKILL.md and mcp.json is already a category), and design-system skills like stitch-skills for other vertical tools.
- **Exposed LLM gateways are the new open S3 buckets.** The ARTEX key-theft loop creates demand for a cheap external scanner and a "key firewall": spend anomaly alerts, auto-rotation, and default-credential checks for LiteLLM, Dify and One API. Sell it to indie teams running self-hosted gateways, who are now the target.
