## Ways to capitalize

- **Kolibri is a distribution wedge, not a benchmark trophy.** Apache 2.0 + 3.46B active means a German public-sector or Mittelstand deploy can leave the Mistral/OpenAI meter. Build the missing adapter: vLLM parser + document-ingestion skill that forces “I don’t know” when the retrieved page does not contain the answer, sold as a sovereign appliance. Who loses: EU inference resellers whose margin was language coverage, not model quality.
- **Tool registries that start disabled are the enterprise SKU.** If Uber’s shape is real, the product to ship is not another MCP server. It is the registry-plus-owner-enablement layer: crawl IDL, register tools off, require a named human to flip them, log the actor chain. Ad-hoc `npx` servers lose the next audit.
- **Sell curation, not skills.** 90k SKILL.md files is a junk drawer. A signed, versioned skill index with conflict tests (two skills giving opposite house rules) is the 1997 Yahoo for procedures. book-to-skill’s token claim is the pricing story: distill once, query many, charge for the distillation not the tokens.
- **Destructive-tool preview as a default primitive.** Copy Cycle’s two-call commit (preview IDs, second call must echo them) into every agent that can touch prod, billing, or email. The harness that refuses to send is the one enterprises will install; the one that “just does it” gets blocked.
- **Do not build another agent video marketplace on a single receipt.** Price anchors without a quality grader are marketing. Spend the cycle on identity + escrow that withholds payment until a check the buyer did not have to watch.
