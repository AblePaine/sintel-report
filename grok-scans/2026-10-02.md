## Ways to capitalize

- **Decision layer is now a commodity.** Cloudflare just open-sourced the thing indie stacks were paying Jev/TypeSafe for: typed option + probability, one forward pass. Build the missing piece they did not ship — domain schemas and eval sets (support triage, procurement approval, coding-agent tool choice, payment authorization). Whoever owns the labeled decision traces owns the fine-tune; the 27B backbone is free. Displaced: per-call decision APIs and any "coordinator agent" that spends frontier tokens to pick the next specialist.

- **Coding-agent distribution just decoupled from the lab.** Cursor routing GLM 5.3 Max as top open-weight on their own bench means the IDE is the customer, the model is a line item. Ship skills/rules/MCP servers that assume a cheap open coder as the default and a frontier model only for the hard step. Price the product on outcomes, not tokens. Displaced: seat-based copilots priced as if every completion were Opus-class.

- **Controllable image edit, before the weights drop.** FLUX 3 commercial weights are the window. Build the agent-facing edit surface (bbox layout, reference lock, multi-turn diff) as an MCP tool now, on BFL's commercial API, and swap to self-host when open weights land. The leverage is the workflow (brand system, catalog, ad variant), not the model. Displaced: human retouchers and closed edit APIs that cannot guarantee untouched pixels.

- **Robot policy from web video is a data business, not a model business.** Praxis-1 is partner-gated for months. The scarce input is embodiment-paired video with action labels, not another VLA. Collect or license task video on hardware the three named partners are not covering (specific warehouse SKUs, lab instruments, home appliances) and sell the dataset or the adapter. Displaced: teleop farms if the transfer numbers hold; ignore if they do not — weights are not public, so do not bet the company on the blog post.

- **Agent labor needs a settlement primitive, not another chat UI.** Liege's split (offline shell, online wallet/MCP approval) is the right shape. Copy the split, not the token: an agent job board where MCP approval and USDC/x402 settlement are the product, and the model is bring-your-own. Displaced: human freelance marketplaces on micro-tasks an agent can escrow and verify.
