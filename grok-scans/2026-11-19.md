## Ways to capitalize

- **The bank-agent crackdown is the opening for a compliance layer.** Arizona's criminal probe, plus banks already blocking third-party agents, means "agent touched my bank account" is now legal exposure. Build the sanctioned path: a bank-side agent gateway with scoped OAuth-style mandates, per-action consent, an immutable audit log and kill switches. Sell it to banks as the way to *allow* agents. Displaces: screen-scraping agent fintechs, which get shut out.
- **Messaging is the agent distribution channel, and Linq just became the Twilio of it.** A $1B valuation on 1B messages, DoorDash checkout in iMessage, and calling plus computer use on top. Build the vertical agents that live entirely in a text thread (local services, booking, reorders) on Linq or Apple MSP rails instead of apps. Displaces: app-install funnels and SMS marketing tools.
- **Open robot policies make the integrator the business.** Praxis-1 is commercial-use, LoRA-fine-tunable on one 80GB GPU and runs on Jetson Thor. The money moves to whoever owns task data and deployment: a "fine-tune your arm in 30 minutes of demos" service for SMB factories and warehouses, sold per cell. Displaces: closed-policy robotics startups charging license fees.
- **Curated MCP is the new app store chokepoint.** GitHub verifying connectors means unverified servers lose distribution in Copilot. If you run an MCP server, get verified now. If you're building, the cross-client verification and badge registry (Copilot, Claude, Cursor, Codex) is still unowned.
- **Cheap open document extraction kills per-page IDP pricing.** Cloudflare Clairic-flash at 9B is self-hostable. Wrap it as a vertical extractor (insurance claims, freight bills, K-1s) with a validation UI and price per workflow, not per page.
- **Anthropic's IPO plus the MisAnthropic coalition makes "provably safe" a sellable product.** With safety branding under public attack on day one of trading, third-party attestations (independent evals, RSP-compliance audits, incident disclosure) have a buyer in every enterprise procurement team that has to justify a Claude contract.
