## Ways to capitalize
- **Government agent marketplaces are the new GSA schedule.** FedAI is a whitelist: once you're in the cohort, you sell agents into 80k seats. The leverage for builders is *compliance-wrapped agent skills* (Arabic-first, audit-logged, sovereign-hosted) sold through G42/Core42 as the prime contractor. The losers are generic SaaS sold seat-by-seat to ministries.
- **Compute is turning into a credit product.** Banks offloading AI loans, LIAN going public in London, and Nvidia chips showing up where they shouldn't: compute risk is being repriced and moved around. There's an opening for anyone who can *underwrite GPU collateral* (utilization proofs, location attestation, chain-of-custody for accelerators). Export-compliance attestation could become a sellable API for lenders and neoclouds.
- **Open 708B MIT weights cut the floor out of "sovereign AI" pricing.** Any country or regulated sector that wanted a non-US model now has a free one. The money moves to fine-tuning, eval and hosting services for these models in non-US clouds, not to the model itself.
- **Teleop-first humanoids = a data-labor market.** At 80 ms full-body teleop, remote operators can do real work and generate training data at the same time. You could build the staffing, QA and data-licensing layer, an "Upwork for robot pilots".
- **Chains are shipping their own agent IDEs** (Solana Playground). Distribution for crypto dev tools is going to the L1 foundations. Indie devs should ship *skills/templates* into these surfaces rather than competing standalone IDEs.
