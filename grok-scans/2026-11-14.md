## Ways to capitalize

- **Agent checkout is splitting into two webs.** OpenAI built tokenized agent payments with the card networks the same day United made agent bookings a contract violation. The leverage is the merchant-side "agent door": a sanctioned endpoint (MCP or API) where a merchant accepts verified agents with network tokens, scoped permissions and liability rules, instead of blocking all of them. Sell it to airlines, ticketing and retail as fraud reduction, not as AI. Scrapers and screen-driving agents get displaced first.
- **Agent traces are the new secrets leak.** Cursor's breach ran through training traces with unredacted keys, then a marketplace cache. Build trace hygiene as a product: redact credentials at capture, sign and pin extension and skill artifacts, and prove what an agent touched. Agent Sentry and the UniGetUI skills manager show both ends of the market forming; the gap is a signed, audited skill/extension registry for teams.
- **AI capex is now a credit story.** The Fed puts AI-compute debt at $680B issued this year and over $1.1T outstanding, with leases and guarantees hard to see. Builders who can map who owes whom (lease commitments, backstops, take-or-pay terms per neocloud) have a data product lenders and LPs will pay for before Nov 17.
- **Harness beats model on cost.** Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 tie at the top, and Reason claims 20x cheaper per task with the same models. Margin is moving to routing, caching and verification layers. Build per-task cost and pass-rate dashboards on top of agent harnesses; teams paying frontier prices on every call are the customers.
