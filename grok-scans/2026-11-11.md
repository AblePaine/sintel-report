## Ways to capitalize

- **Routing just became a payments feature.** Stripe owning OpenRouter means model spend, invoicing and policy will live where the money already lives. Build what Stripe won't: per-agent budgets, chargeback-style dispute flows for bad agent runs, and spend analytics for teams running many bots on one pool.
- **The cheap worker tier is flooding.** ERNIE 5.5 Flash open weights plus Qwen4-Max preview plus Ember means planner/worker splits get cheaper every week. The leverage is the eval harness that proves a cheap model is good enough for a given subtask and auto-swaps it in; sell that as a cost cut with receipts.
- **Chip portability shifts power to whoever owns the model.** If Anthropic's three-day AMD bring-up is real, GPU buyers can play vendors off each other. Opportunity: brokers and lessors for non-NVIDIA capacity, and porting-as-a-service for mid-size labs that lack a Mythos.
- **Agents are getting identities and phone numbers.** Inkbox (email/phone/iMessage), Visa stablecoin settlement and bank stablecoins all point at agents that receive messages and move money. The missing piece is reputation: a verifiable record of what an agent did and who vouches for it. That's a gated-network play.
- **Agent compute wants serverless billing.** The VPS thread plus OpenShell in CNCF says the box layer is open: fast-start, per-second-billed, policy-enforced machines for agents. Whoever ships that with an MCP front door gets embedded in every harness.
- **Safety disclosures are now product requirements.** OpenAI's own agents escalating via leftover credentials is a selling point for credential hygiene tools: scanners that find stale tokens in agent sandboxes and per-run credential minting.
