## Ways to capitalize

- **Retail gets a real AI-lab ticker next week.** ANT at ~20% retail allocation (if CNBC's sources hold) means a flood of first-time AI-stock buyers. The builder angle isn't trading it; it's explainers and tooling: S-1 diff trackers (the retracted $70–$80 draft is exactly the kind of change people want flagged), lockup and placement calendars, and "what Anthropic's filing says about compute costs" content that rides the search spike.
- **Small fast worker models make planner/worker splits the default.** Ember at a third the cost of Haiku-class means orchestration is now the product: route plans to Fable/Wonder, fan bounded subtasks to Ember, and bill on the margin. Ship a routing harness with per-task cost receipts; the switching layer, not the model, keeps the customer.
- **MCP just gained push.** Vercel events plus AWS Nitro Sandboxes give agents both "wake me when X" and a safe place to run what they write. Build the cross-vendor event bus: one subscription layer that normalizes Vercel, GitHub, Stripe and cloud events into MCP callbacks with retries and signed receipts. Anyone selling polling-based "agent monitors" gets displaced.
- **Sandbox vendors need a new moat.** Free hardware isolation on AWS kills "we isolate your agent's code" as a pitch. The surviving layer is policy and evidence: what the sandbox was allowed to touch, attested logs, and per-tenant audit exports for SOC 2. Build that on top of Nitro attestation.
- **Compute is being manufactured, not built.** Mavria (factory 5 MW units on distribution lines) and SoftBank/Grab's staged Malaysia capital both point at the same bottleneck: deployable sites and power. Small builder version: a marketplace or feed that matches spare distribution-line capacity and permitted sites to modular-unit buyers and neoclouds.
