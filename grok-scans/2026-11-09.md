## Ways to capitalize

- **Confidential inference is now a cloud SKU, not a research topic.** TPU 8tx makes "the cloud can't see your weights or prompts" a buyable line item. Build the attestation-and-audit layer on top: a service that verifies the enclave, logs every key release, and hands a compliance officer a signed report per inference batch. Sell to health, legal and gov agent builders who've been stuck on-prem.
- **MCP gateways are the new API gateway market.** MCP1, AWS AgentCore Gateway and others are converging on one URL, per-tool policy, audit. The gap is cross-vendor policy as code: a portable YAML policy that compiles to MCP1, AgentCore and self-hosted gateways, plus a "what did my agents touch this week" report. Whoever owns the policy file owns the switching cost.
- **Agent identity plus wallets is being productized from the crypto side.** Animoca (AIR credentials, Minds agents) and Formation (KYC-gated wallets trading tokenized equities 24/7) together sketch the rail: verified agent identity, permissioned wallet, round-the-clock settlement. Build the permission broker in between: spending limits, allowlisted pools, human approval above thresholds, revocation. Brokers and robo-advisers that assume market hours get displaced.
- **Plugin economies beat skill files for distribution.** DeepSeek Harness has 35K plugins and is about to pay authors in tokens. An indie dev with a good Claude/Codex skill should port it to a DSH plugin now, while the marketplace is new and featured slots are cheap, and use the same code as an MCP server for Western users.
- **Behind-the-meter power is the real AI bottleneck trade.** Anthropic buying stranded generator orders shows labs will pay for queue position. Builders with capital or ops chops: aggregate in-flight turbine/generator orders and permits from undercapitalized developers and resell positions to labs and neoclouds. Small-scale version: software that tracks generator lead times and order transfers as a market feed.
