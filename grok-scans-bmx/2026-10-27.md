## Ways to capitalize
- **The S-1 makes usage-based AI revenue legible to public markets.** 900% NRR means customers expand spend as agents do more work. Build for the buyer side of that curve: per-team agent spend controls, chargeback and forecasting for Claude/Codex usage. FinOps tools built for cloud VMs get displaced by ones that meter tokens and agent tasks.
- **Compute is now financed like aircraft leasing.** Broadcom's SPV and SpaceX's lease both put chips in vehicles with residual-value guarantees. Expect a secondary market in off-lease accelerators from 2028 on; the leverage is in residual-value modeling, re-lease brokering and cheap inference on second-life racks.
- **Cheap computer use kills the "integration moat."** Terra at a tenth of Astra's price makes it economical to drive any legacy GUI. Pick a vertical with ugly portals (claims, freight, county records), sell per completed task, and price under the BPO contractor it replaces. Pair it with Cursor-style agents-as-code so the workflow is versioned and eval'd.
- **Agent payments need volume, not more rails.** At 5 cents a payment and $3.7M a quarter, the bottleneck is things worth buying. Build paid endpoints agents actually need (data lookups, verification, small compute) priced per call, and leave the rails war to Mastercard, Coinbase and the stablecoin issuers.
