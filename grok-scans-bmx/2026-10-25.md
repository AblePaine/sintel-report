## Ways to capitalize
- **Bank-sanctioned agent access is the new Plaid moment.** Banks are blocking credential-sharing agents but blessing read-only access through approved providers. Build the approved, scoped, auditable agent-to-bank connector (read first, then limited write with human limits). Screen-scraping agent fintechs get displaced.
- **x402 has rails but no revenue yet; sell what sits above the rails.** $120k across 6.4M payments says the market is sub-cent API calls. The money is in metering, spend limits, refunds and dispute evidence for agent payments ("charged means delivered"), and in credit and reputation scores like Agentics Credit, applied beyond trading agents.
- **Local open models as the escape hatch from lab policy changes.** The REA restriction is the template: every time a lab narrows a use, demand shifts to local models behind the same MCP tools. A "bring your own local model" wrapper for popular MCP servers, with Flint-class coders and Kimi weights on Nov 4, is a clean wedge.
- **Inference arbitrage is a land grab with a short half-life.** Margins come from GPU leases bought early plus cheap open models. Builders without leases should sell the layer the arbitrageurs need: routing across many inference providers by price and quality, or utilization tools that keep rented GPUs above break-even.
- **Voice agents without the LLM pipeline.** If Dessa-style speech-to-speech holds up, latency-sensitive phone agents (support, booking, intake) get rebuilt; current ASR plus LLM plus TTS vendors are exposed. The flip side is the fraud wave already in the voice-cloning story, so caller verification and family or enterprise code-phrase products have a real buyer.
