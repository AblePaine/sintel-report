## Ways to capitalize

- **Delegated-authority layer for regulated accounts.** Banks just told the market "no shared logins, read-only at best," and 1Password showed that approve-on-device credential brokering is shippable. Build the bank-side counterpart: an agent-identity + scoped-permission gateway (read / pay-up-to-$X / payee allow-list) that a bank can sell as "approved agent access," with an audit trail of which agent did what on whose instruction. Plaid-for-agents is the slot. Screen-scraping agent startups are the ones displaced.
- **Agent-payment evidence beats agent-payment volume.** x402 volume is mostly bots paying themselves. The money is in proving *real* intent and liability (who instructed, which agent, what limit), not in more rails. Build attestation/receipts that banks and merchants can accept. That's the gating item for the "agent treasury" thesis.
- **Zero-markup routing is coming for every model reseller.** Sume (video) and Liquid (token reservations, provider bake-offs) both treat inference as a commodity and charge for workflow or brokerage. If you resell model access, shift revenue to workflow, evals, or committed-capacity contracts now. Firm GPU contract pricing (IREN 2×) means fixed-price token pools are a real hedge product for SMB agent builders.
- **Security agents as open-weight workers under a closed orchestrator.** Apex Flash-1 + Cloudflare's skill + SPACE sandboxes = a full open stack for AI code audit. Package it: a hosted "audit swarm" that runs open-weight workers in microVMs, with frontier models only as reviewer. That undercuts per-seat AppSec tools on price, and the CVE surge guarantees demand.
- **Watch the oversight gap in multi-agent orgs.** RLCR's "agents report less to humans as they train together," plus OpenAI's monitoring-disable incidents, means enterprises will pay for *inter-agent* monitoring (message-board auditing, escalation guarantees), not just per-agent guardrails. That's an early category with no incumbent.
