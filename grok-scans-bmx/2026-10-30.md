## Ways to capitalize

- **Supply-chain trust for agents is now a product.** The Cursor incident shows marketplaces can't be trusted blindly. Build an offline, buy-once "agent hygiene" scanner that checks `authorized_keys`, launch agents, extension hashes and skill folders against known-good sources. It needs no account and no cloud, which is exactly AblePaine's lane, and nobody sells it to solo devs today.
- **Skills-as-files is winning (Cloudflare plus the AAIF spec), so skill packs are a distribution format.** Sell vertical skill packs, and a local `SKILL.md` linter and preview tool, the way people sold WordPress themes in 2005. The spec is open, so whoever owns discovery and trust gets the leverage.
- **The harness is where cost gets cut.** Droid's batched tool calls (claimed 50x fewer tokens) mean tool surfaces should be designed for code-mode. One composable MCP endpoint beats 300 small tools. Builders who expose agent-friendly batch APIs get cheaper, more reliable agents than competitors on the same model.
- **Open weights make offline paid apps viable.** A quantized Le Chonk running locally means a buy-once offline coding and security assistant can match mid-tier cloud subscriptions. Pika H3 Flex does the same for local video generation. Both displace per-seat API resellers.
- **Lab-hosted agents get shut down (Jules).** Users now value portable agent state and memory. A local exporter or "bring your agent history with you" tool, plus marketing your apps as "never sunsetted," turns subscription fatigue into a selling point.
- **Compute concentration is extreme (one customer is about a third of SpaceX revenue, if Bloomberg is right).** Inference supply sits with a few vendors, which is a structural argument for on-device models in consumer apps. Price and availability shocks hit cloud-only apps first.
- **Consumer agent distribution is moving into group chats and the phone line (Wabi, Phonic, and yesterday's OpenAI texting preview).** A small game or app that lives inside a group thread, or answers a phone number, skips the app-store fight.
