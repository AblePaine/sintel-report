## Ways to capitalize
- **MCP just became a distribution channel, not a dev toy.** Google letting third-party apps show up inside Gemini and Search over MCP is the 1997 "get listed in Yahoo" moment. For an offline, buy-once studio, the play is a thin MCP surface on each app (local-first, user-approved actions) so it's callable from Gemini without giving up ownership of the app. Early listings get the default slots before the shelf fills up.
- **The model is becoming an alias.** Vercel virtual models, Copilot Fusion's router and Particle-1 on OpenRouter all push the same way: agents call a name, and the platform picks the model on cost and quality. Lock-in moves from the lab to the router. Builder angle: route per task inside your own tools, and price your product on outcomes, not on one vendor's tokens.
- **Agent infra is chasing CI and compute waste.** Vercel (skip unchanged work) and Amp (rent a Mac only 35% of the time) both sell "the agent does less, cheaper." A narrow version for game builds (incremental asset and shader builds that agents can trigger) is an open niche.
- **Physical AI money keeps flowing to "factory as a service."** Foundry sells the output of robot cells instead of the robots. The same model works small: sell finished units built by automation, not the automation.
- **Optics is the next AI bottleneck to own.** If Marvell–Lumentum closes, interconnect consolidates into fewer hands. Not a builder play for a small studio, but a signal to watch for GPU and cloud pricing in 2027.
